About Fit Deal

See an outfit you love? We find it, for less.

Fit Deal turns a screenshot or a product link into real products you can buy in India, and shows you where they cost the least.

Honest ranking

The closest match and the better price go first. What a store pays us never decides the order.

Your photos stay yours

A screenshot is used once to find matches and is never stored, sold or used to train AI.

No made-up numbers

We only call something an exact match when we can prove it, and we don't show fake match scores or reviews.

Why we built it

You see an outfit you love in a reel or a pin, but there's no name and no link. When you finally find something close, it costs far more than you wanted to spend.

Fit Deal fixes that in one step. Upload the screenshot, and we recognise the clothing, search the big Indian stores, and line up the same product and similar ones by price. You buy directly from the store, at the store's normal price.

Fit Deal is an independent project made in India. It isn't owned by or connected to any store.
