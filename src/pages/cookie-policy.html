In short

  • Fit Deal does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • We keep a small sign-in cookie only if you create an account, and your saved items, recent searches and preferences in your browser's own local storage.
  • Everything stored on your device can be cleared from the Profile page or your browser settings.

What we store

  • Sign-in cookie (fd_token): set only when you sign in, so you stay signed in for 30 days. SameSite=Lax, path /.
  • Local storage on your device: saved items (fd_saved_v1), recent searches (fd_recent_v1), votes and size notes. This never leaves your browser unless you sign in and choose to sync.
  • Session storage: temporary search state while you use the photo finder.

We do not store your uploaded photos: they are processed once in memory and discarded, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Why we store it

  • To keep you signed in, if you have an account.
  • To remember your saved looks, recent searches and size notes on your device.
  • To count page views and fix errors (anonymous, aggregated analytics only).

Third-party cookies

Fit Deal sets no third-party cookies. When you tap “Buy”, you leave for the retailer's own website (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra or AJIO), which applies its own cookie policy — we have no control over cookies set there.

Your control

  • Sign out any time from the Profile page to delete the sign-in cookie.
  • Use “Clear” on the Profile page to remove saved items, searches and votes from your device.
  • Your browser settings can block or delete cookies and site data for fitdeal.shop at any time. Blocking the sign-in cookie only signs you out; the site otherwise keeps working.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us.
