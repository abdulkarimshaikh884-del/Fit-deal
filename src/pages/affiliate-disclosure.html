In short

Fit Deal is free because stores may pay us a small commission when you buy through our links. You pay the same price. The commission never decides which products you see or their order.

How we earn

When you tap “Buy” on Fit Deal and then buy something at the store, the store or an affiliate network may pay us a percentage of the sale. If you return the order, or the store cancels it, we don't earn from it.

Programmes we use

  • Amazon Associates. Fit Deal is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program for Amazon.in. As an Amazon Associate, Fit Deal earns from qualifying purchases.
  • Flipkart Affiliate Program.
  • Affiliate networks (such as Cuelinks) for stores like Myntra and AJIO.

Not every store or product earns a commission. We show products whether or not we earn from them.

Ranking is never for sale

Results are ordered only by how closely they match what you searched for and by price. Exact matches come first, cheapest first; similar products are grouped by how close they are, cheapest first in each group. Our ranking code doesn't even receive commission rates.

Sponsored results

Fit Deal has no paid placements today. If we ever add them, they'll be clearly labelled “Sponsored” and kept separate from the normal results.

How our links work

Buy buttons go through a Fit Deal address (fitdeal.shop/go/…) that adds our affiliate code and sends you straight on to the store's product page. We count the click (which product and store, never who you are) so we can match it to the store's commission reports.
