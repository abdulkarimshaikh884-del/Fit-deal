FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find your answer? Write to us.

Do I need to know the brand or product name?

No. A clear screenshot is enough. If there's a lot else in the picture, crop it to the outfit first.

What's the difference between Exact and Similar?

Exact means we can prove it's the same product: it's the product from your link, or it has the same barcode, or the same brand and style code. Similar means it looks alike but may be a different product. We don't show match percentages, because a number we can't back up would only mislead you.

Will I always find the exact product?

No. Many outfits in reels are custom, old, or not sold online. From a screenshot we usually show similar products; exact matches are most likely when you paste a product link. When we can't confirm a product is the same, we say Similar.

Which stores do you search?

Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra and AJIO. You can also paste product links from these four stores. We'll add more stores over time.

How do you decide the order of results?

Exact matches come first, cheapest first. Similar products are grouped by how closely they match, and inside each group the cheaper one goes first. How much a store pays us is never part of the order.

Why is the price at the store different?

Stores change prices and stock often. Every price shows when we checked it. The store's page always has the final price, including delivery, offers and coupons.

What happens to my photos?

Your photo is sent once to our image-recognition service to identify the clothing, then discarded. We don't store it, share it publicly, sell it, or use it to train AI. We keep only the text description (for example “women's pink floral midi dress”) and the results. Read the privacy policy.

Does it cost anything? How do you make money?

Fit Deal is free. When you buy through our links, the store or an affiliate network may pay us a small commission. You pay the same price. See our affiliate disclosure.

Do I need an account? Where are my saved items?

No account needed. Saved items stay in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser data, they're removed.

How does “Help me choose” work?

Pick 2 or 3 products from your results and we make a vote page. Share the link on WhatsApp; friends tap the one they like, no sign-in needed. Votes are open for 7 days.

A result is wrong. How do I tell you?

Tap “Wrong match?” under the product. We read every report and use them to fix our matching.

How do I delete my data?

Saved items: remove them on the Saved page. Searches, votes or messages: write to privacy@fitdeal.shop or use the contact form (topic “Delete my data”) with the link, and we'll delete it within 7 days.
