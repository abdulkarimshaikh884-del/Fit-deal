How it works

From screenshot to best price in 4 simple steps

Saw an outfit on Instagram, YouTube or Pinterest? You don't need the brand name. A screenshot is enough.

  1. Step 1

    Upload

    Add a screenshot or photo of the outfit, or paste a product link from Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra or AJIO.

  2. Step 2

    Find

    Our AI spots each piece of clothing: type, colour, pattern and fit. If there's more than one, you pick which to search for. Then we search the stores.

  3. Step 3

    Compare

    See the exact product (when we can confirm it) and similar look-alikes, with each store's price and when we checked it.

  4. Step 4

    Buy

    Tap “Buy” to go to the store's own page and buy there. You pay the store's normal price; payment, delivery and returns are with the store.

Exact or Similar?

Exact means we have proof it's the same product: it's the product from your link, it has the same barcode, or the same brand and style code.

Similar means it looks alike (same kind of clothing, colour and style) but it may be a different product. We'd rather show fewer “Exact” labels than a wrong one.

Why is Fit Deal free?

When you buy through a link on Fit Deal, the store or its affiliate network may pay us a small commission. You pay exactly the same price.

  • The closest match and better price go first, whatever the commission
  • No extra charges, ever
  • We never sell your photos or personal data
