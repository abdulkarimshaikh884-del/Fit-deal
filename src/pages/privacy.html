In short

  • Your screenshot is used once to recognise the clothing, then discarded. We never store your photos.
  • We never use your photos to train AI. If that ever changes, it will only be with your explicit opt-in.
  • No account, no cookies from us, no advertising trackers.
  • We don't sell personal data.

Fit Deal (“we”, “us”) helps you find clothing from screenshots and product links and compare prices across online stores in India. This policy explains what we process and why, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Your photos

When you upload a screenshot or photo, your browser first shrinks it and removes hidden details such as location data. We then send it once to our image-recognition provider, Google (the Gemini API, a paid service), to identify the clothing in it. The photo is handled in memory and is not saved on our servers or in our database.

Under Google's terms for its paid Gemini API, Google does not use what we send to improve its products, and may keep it for a limited time only to detect abuse. We keep only the text result, for example “women's pink floral midi dress”, and the products we found.

Please upload only photos you're allowed to use. Don't upload photos of other people that show their face without their permission.

What we keep

  • Searches: the recognised description, any product link you pasted, and the products and prices we found. This makes your results link work when you share it.
  • Clicks to stores: which product and store you clicked, so we can check affiliate reports. No name, no IP address.
  • Usage statistics: pages viewed and steps completed (for example “search finished”), with a visitor code that changes every day and can't be traced back to you. We don't store your IP address.
  • Votes: the products you put in a “Help me choose” vote, the first name you chose to show (optional), and anonymous votes.
  • Messages: your name, email and message when you contact us.
  • Reports: when you tell us a result is wrong.

How long we keep it

  • Photos: not kept at all.
  • Searches and votes: 90 days, then deleted.
  • Clicks to stores: 24 months, because affiliate networks confirm or cancel commissions for months after a purchase.
  • Usage statistics: 13 months.
  • Messages: 12 months after we last replied.

Who processes data

We use service providers who process data only to run Fit Deal: Google (image recognition), Render (hosting), Supabase (database), and product-data providers such as the Flipkart Affiliate API, the Amazon Creators API and a Google Shopping search provider. Product searches send them only the search words, never your photo or personal details. We don't sell or rent personal data.

Cookies and storage

Fit Deal doesn't set cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps your saved items, your recent searches and a random code that stops you voting twice in a vote. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of the normal web request.

Store links

When you tap “Buy”, you go to the store through our link. The store and its affiliate partners may set their own cookies to record that you came from Fit Deal, so we can earn a commission. Their privacy policies apply on their sites.

Your rights and deletion

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent. Saved items and recent searches are on your device: remove them on the Saved page. For a search, vote or message, write to privacy@fitdeal.shop or use the contact form with the topic “Delete my data” and the Fit Deal link. We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

Children

Fit Deal is meant for adults. If you're under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian.

Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, write to privacy@fitdeal.shop. We reply within 7 days. If you're not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
