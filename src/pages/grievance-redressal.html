Raise a grievance

If something on Fit Deal is wrong — a misleading result, a bad price, a broken store link, or a concern about your data — tell us through the contact form (choose “Copyright or takedown” or “General question”) or email support@fitdeal.shop with the subject “Grievance”.

Please include: what happened, the page or product link, and the date. Screenshots help us fix things faster.

Response timelines

  • We acknowledge every grievance within 24 hours.
  • We resolve it within 15 days, and tell you the outcome.
  • Copyright or takedown requests are reviewed promptly; content found to infringe is removed.

Grievance officer

In line with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021:

  • Name: Fit Deal Grievance Officer
  • Email: support@fitdeal.shop (subject: “Grievance Officer”)
  • Address: Mumbai, Maharashtra, India

Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the appropriate consumer forum under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, or the National Consumer Helpline (1800-11-4000).

Fit Deal is a price-comparison service: your purchase, payment, delivery and returns are handled by the retailer you buy from (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra or AJIO). For order issues, please also contact the retailer's own support.
