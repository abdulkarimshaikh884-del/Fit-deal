About Fit Deal

Fit Deal (fitdeal.shop) is an independent Indian fashion price-comparison platform. Shoppers upload a screenshot or paste a product link; Fit Deal finds the same or similar clothing across Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra and AJIO and shows the best price. Affiliate commissions never change the ranking.

Press contact

For interviews, quotes or background: support@fitdeal.shop with the subject “Press”. We usually reply within two business days.
